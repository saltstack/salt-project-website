## What are RSS Feeds?

RSS feeds (Really Simple Syndication) are an easy way to keep up with Salt Project news and information. An RSS feed contains headlines, summaries, and links to full content. RSS is written in XML (eXtensible Markup Language).

You can view feeds with an RSS reader or aggregator. Some browsers, such as current versions of Firefox and Safari, have built-in RSS readers. If your browser doesn't support RSS, there are many RSS readers available on the web.
